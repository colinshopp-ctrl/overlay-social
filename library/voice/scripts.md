# Voiceover scripts

What Sedona says in each voiced Reel of `library/2026-12`, one sentence per render (see `README.md`).

## 01-aspects

- Aspects are the angles between planets, and each one asks something different of you.
- A conjunction blends two planets, so they act as one.
- A sextile is an opening, if you take it.
- A square is friction, the kind that gets things moving.
- A trine is ease. It flows without trying.
- And an opposition is a tug of war, and a mirror.
- In synastry, the same angles run between your chart and your partner's.

## 03-houses

- The twelve houses are areas of life, measured from the moment and place you were born.
- They run from the self, and how you begin, all the way round to rest, and what's hidden.
- In a composite chart, they show where the relationship lives its life.

## 10-seventh-house

- The seventh house is the house of partners: who you're drawn to, and how you meet them.

- And in a composite chart, a busy seventh house means the partnership itself is the point.

## 12-three-charts

- Two people can have three charts together.
- The composite is the relationship itself, made from the midpoints of your two charts.
- Synastry lays your two charts over each other, to show how you affect one another.
- And the Davison chart gives the relationship its own birthday, halfway between yours.
- Each one answers a different question, and Overlay draws all three.

## 14-retrograde

- What does retrograde actually mean?
- Every so often, a planet seems to stop, then slide backward across the sky.
- It isn't really moving backward. Earth is overtaking it, like passing a slower car.
- Astrologers read it as a time to review what that planet rules, rather than start something new.
