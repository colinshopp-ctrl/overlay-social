# December–January videos

Thirty vertical videos (1080×1920), made ahead for the launch plan on 2026-11-09. Seventeen are voiced (marked 🎙 below; 20 to 39 seconds, the words on screen timed to the voice, see `../voice/`). The other thirteen are 15 seconds, captions only, with a silent AAC track for a sound picked in Metricool.

The weekly social task draws on these: each week, use the videos whose date falls in that week, adapt the caption and add the usual tags. Add a trending sound only to the captions-only videos: voiced ones go out with their own audio, nothing under the voice. Dates are suggestions (Central); keep them near the sky events they belong to. Videos dated January 1 or later end on "Get started free"; earlier ones on "Join the waitlist".

Rituals and the first Moon Journey aren't in the app yet; their videos come once they ship.
